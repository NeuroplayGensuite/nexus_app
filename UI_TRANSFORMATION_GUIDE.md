### 1. Animated Background System
- **Gradient Animation**: 15s infinite color shift
- **Particle System**: 20 floating orbs with random timing
- **Depth Layers**: Multi-layer parallax effect

### 2. Glassmorphism Cards

background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05-0.1)
backdrop-filter: blur(20px-30px)

### 3. Neon Glow Effects
- **Blue Glow**: Status indicators, sync badges
- **Purple Glow**: Progress bars, profile cards
- **Pink Glow**: Special badges, achievements

### 4. Game Card Interactions
\\\
Hover: translateY(-12px) scale(1.02)
Icon: rotate(12deg) scale(1.25)
Play Button: Appears with fade + scale
Completion Badge: Bounce animation
\\\
